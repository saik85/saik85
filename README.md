## 🧭 About Me

I design and modernize **cloud-native data platforms** for regulated industries — carrying data from legacy Informatica/Oracle warehousing into modern **lakehouse engineering** on AWS, GCP, and Azure. I build streaming pipelines with exactly-once guarantees, change data capture, dimensional models, and governed data products, and I set platform standards and mentor engineering teams.

**Where I've built, across 5 industries:**

- 🏥 **HCA Healthcare** — leading a **clinical data lakehouse** on AWS Databricks (190 hospitals, ~2,500 sites of care, ~47M encounters/yr)
- 🏦 **First Citizens Bank** — real-time **fraud & payments** platform (150M+ events/month) with a 2-minute risk-decision SLA
- 🛒 **Costco** — **retail & supply-chain analytics** on GCP across 800+ warehouses (50M daily transactions)
- 🛡️ **Brown & Brown** — **insurance** ETL & reconciliation, surfacing premium leakage
- 📡 **Cox Communications** — **telecom** subscriber & billing data warehouse powering churn, ARPU & retention
