ABOUT

From data warehousing to cloud-native lakehouse.

Over 12+ years I've designed, built, and modernized enterprise data platforms across healthcare, banking, retail, insurance, and telecom — carrying data from Informatica/Oracle warehousing into cloud-native lakehouse engineering on AWS, GCP, and Azure.

My focus is the hard part of production data: real-time streaming with exactly-once guarantees, change data capture that keeps platforms current without full reloads, dimensional modeling for trustworthy analytics, and governance for regulated data — HIPAA-protected PHI, fraud, and AML datasets.

Alongside the engineering I set platform standards for architecture, governance, and pipeline design, and mentor the engineers who build on them.
